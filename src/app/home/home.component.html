Pulsed, High-Voltage Systems, Components, and Diagnostics:

Solutions for Research, Engineering, and Technology

L-Egant Solutions sells a broad range of mostly-custom, specialized-high-voltage systems, components, and diagnositcs for research, commercial, and defense applications. Our high voltage systems and product lines include: pulse generators (Marx banks, Pulse Forming Networks, trigger generators, fuse triggers, etc.), H.V. charging supplies, high-energy discharge switches (spark gaps, rail gaps, gas filled gaps, etc.), H.V. diodes, capacitors, resistors.
